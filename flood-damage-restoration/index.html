When Floodwater Is an Emergency

If water is coming into your Beverly Hills home or garage right now, treat it as an emergency. Our line is open 24/7, and the person who answers will help you decide what to do while a crew is arranged. Shut off the main water valve if the source is inside the house, lift valuables and electronics off the floor, and keep people and pets out of any water that may carry sewage or runoff.

Electrical and gas safety come first

  • ✓Do not wade through standing water near outlets, panels or appliances
  • ✓Cut power only if you can reach the panel while standing on dry ground
  • ✓If water has reached outlets or you smell gas, leave and call 911 or the utility before calling the restoration line
  • ✓Keep children and pets out of the affected rooms until crews confirm they are safe

Flood damage restoration covers the water that arrives from outside or from below: storm runoff, groundwater, failed pumps and sewer backups. It differs from a burst pipe because the water is often Category 2 or 3 under the S500 standard, which changes what can be dried and what has to be removed. For clean water from a broken line, see emergency water removal.

How Beverly Hills Properties Flood

Flooding here rarely looks like a river. It is more often runoff finding the lower level of a hillside property, a garage drain that backs up, a failed sump or ejector pump in a below-grade room, a broken irrigation line, or a utility main that fails. In 2026, a 36-inch trunk line in neighboring West Hollywood sent about 17 million gallons of water down Sunset Boulevard and into underground garages, according to local news reports.

The lesson for Beverly Hills owners is practical. Below-grade garages and lower levels collect water first, so find the pump if you have one and test it, keep drains clear, and know where your shutoff is. To see how your address is classified, use the FEMA Flood Map Service Center, and ask the Beverly Hills Community Development Department about drainage requirements for hillside lots.

Basement Flooding Cleanup

Many Beverly Hills homes have a lower level or basement, and many condo buildings have subterranean parking and storage. Water in these spaces cannot drain away, so it sits and soaks into drywall, framing, stored boxes and carpet. The EPA advises drying water-damaged areas and items within 24 to 48 hours to prevent mold growth, so the clock matters.

The work runs in a set order. Standing water is pumped out, wet carpet and pad come up, saturated drywall is cut above the wet line, and air movers and dehumidifiers dry what remains. If the water came from outside or from a drain, surfaces are cleaned and treated before anything is rebuilt. Restoration is not waterproofing, so if water keeps entering after a dry spell, the entry point needs a separate fix.

Sewage Backup Cleanup: What to Do First

Sewage coming up through a floor drain, shower or toilet is a Category 3 loss. Do not touch it, do not run more water down the drains, and keep everyone out of the room. The EPA says to call in a professional with experience cleaning buildings damaged by contaminated water, and that is the right call here.

Crews extract the contaminated water, remove porous materials such as carpet, pad and soaked drywall, then clean and treat what remains. A plumber or the city can tell you whether the blockage sits in your private line or a public main, which matters for the repair and for any claim.

What Affects the Cost of Flood Damage Restoration

Flood jobs vary more than most water losses because the water source and the space both change the scope. These five factors set most of the cost.

Water category

Category 3 water means more removal and a more involved cleaning plan.

Affected area

A single garage bay and a whole lower level are different jobs.

Materials involved

Carpet, drywall, stored contents and finished floors respond differently to contaminated water.

Drying days and equipment

Lower levels dry slowly, so equipment may run longer.

Rebuild scope

Replacing drywall, flooring and finishes is priced separately.
