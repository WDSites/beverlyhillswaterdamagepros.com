What We Do

Beverly Hills Water Damage Pros connects property owners in Beverly Hills and West Hollywood with licensed and insured restoration professionals for water damage and mold. Our address is 9000 Wilshire Blvd, Beverly Hills, CA 90210, and the emergency line is (424) 550-5662.

The services are water mitigation, emergency water removal, water damage cleanup, flood damage restoration, water damage repair and mold remediation. They are listed in full on the services page.

How the Line Works

You call and describe what happened. The dispatcher asks where the water came from, how long it has been wet, whether the building is a house, condo or commercial space and whether electricity or sewage is involved. You get safety steps for the minutes before help arrives, and when a crew is available, a same-day response is arranged.

Scope and price come first, and the decision to proceed stays with you. The crew keeps moisture readings, photos and a drying log, which give your insurer something concrete to review.

Standards and How to Compare Companies

The ANSI/IICRC S500 water damage restoration standard and the S520 mold remediation standard are published references in the trade. When you compare any restoration company, ask whether its crews work to them, how they document drying and who does the clearance testing for mold. Ask for the license number and verify it at cslb.ca.gov, since Los Angeles County consumer guidance says contractors need a state license for work involving $1,000 or more in labor or materials.

Who Answers and What Happens Next

The line is answered by a dispatcher, who writes down the address, the source and the building type, then gives you safety steps. If a crew is free, it is sent the same day, and you are told what to expect on arrival. If not, you hear that directly, along with what to do until help can reach you.

On site, the crew measures how wet the structure is, explains what needs to come out and what can be dried, and states the price before starting. You decide whether to go ahead, and you are given the readings and photos afterward.

The Kinds of Buildings We See

Beverly Hills mixes single-family homes, older multifamily buildings, high-rise condominiums along Wilshire Boulevard and commercial space. A failure in a house, in a stacked condo and in a ground-floor shop each call for a different plan, and the crew adjusts for access, parking and building rules.

West Hollywood adds dense apartment and condo buildings with shared risers and garages. In both cities, a leak often involves a manager or an association as well as the owner, so we keep records that each of them can follow.

Documentation That Helps Everyone

Water losses end up in conversations with insurers, landlords, associations and sometimes buyers. Moisture readings, dated photos and a drying log answer many of their questions without argument. We keep these records and share them with you.

We do not decide who is responsible for a loss or whether a claim is covered. We explain what we found, and your insurer and the other parties take it from there.

How to Judge Any Restoration Company

  • Ask for the license number and check it with the state board
  • Ask whether crews work to the IICRC S500 and S520 standards
  • Ask how drying is measured and when it is considered finished
  • Ask for the scope and the price in writing before work starts
  • Be careful with anyone who promises results they cannot control

What We Do Not Do

We do not waterproof foundations, repair fire damage, conserve fine art or antiques, or diagnose health effects from mold. If your problem belongs to another trade, we will say so and point you toward the right one. Questions about coverage belong with your insurer, since we cannot promise a claim will be approved.

Where We Work
