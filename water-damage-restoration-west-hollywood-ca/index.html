Which Water Utility Serves Your Address

West Hollywood does not have a single water provider. According to the city, the Los Angeles Department of Water and Power serves most areas of West Hollywood, and Beverly Hills Water serves certain areas on the west side of the city. That matters in an emergency, because a street-side leak or a shutoff at the meter is the utility's job and not ours.

For LADWP service, the city lists 1-800-DIAL-DWP (1-800-342-5397). For the Beverly Hills side, it lists Beverly Hills Public Works Customer Service at (310) 285-2467. If you are not sure which utility serves your building, check the bill or ask management, then call the right one while we handle the inside of the building.

Older Multifamily Buildings and Water That Travels

West Hollywood has a dense mix of older apartment and condo buildings, many with shared walls, stacked bathrooms and plumbing risers that run through several floors. A failed supply line in one unit can drop water through the ceiling below, and a slow drip behind a wall can travel through the cavity before it stains anything.

That is why the first call should include building management, and why photographs and written notes matter. Renters and owners are covered differently, so check your own policy and ask your insurer what it applies to. We document the wet areas with moisture readings, which helps when several parties are involved.

What the 2026 Sunset Boulevard Main Break Taught Owners Here

In 2026, a 36-inch trunk line dating to about 1916 burst on Sunset Boulevard at Holloway Drive. According to news reports, about 17 million gallons flooded the streets, pushed cars across pavement and filled underground garages. Sunset was closed between Sherbourne Drive and San Vicente Boulevard while LADWP repaired the line.

For owners and building managers, the lessons are concrete. Underground garages and storage rooms collect water first. Residents affected by the break were pointed to LADWP's claims page and to the city's recovery information, which shows that a utility failure can lead to its own claims process. Flood damage is usually excluded from standard homeowners policies according to California Department of Insurance guidance, so check your coverage and keep photos and receipts.

Permits and Jurisdiction in West Hollywood

Rebuild work in West Hollywood goes through the city's own Community Development Department, Building and Safety Division, and not through the Beverly Hills Permit Center. The city offers permitting appointments Monday through Friday, and each trade generally needs its own permit. Ask before assuming a repair is exempt, because permit rules vary by scope.

Streets near the city's edges can cross into neighboring jurisdictions. Around the Cedars-Sinai area at the southern edge, West Hollywood meets Los Angeles, so confirm which city's rules apply to your address before planning a rebuild.
